FINDINGS, RANKED
----------------

1. Historical TWS requests produced no bars or END before the watchdog.
What happened: The first request, reqId=2, was issued at 13:22:04.195; pacing continuation issued reqId=5 at 13:22:04.748. At +60 s both were still active with zero bars and no END. At +120.607 s the watchdog canceled both and the cache still held zero bars. The finalizer listed 31 missing trading days. Evidence: reqId=2,5; OBS L32-L37, L56-L58, L79-L80, L100-L111; APP L3644.
Expected behavior: one historicalDataEnd per issued chunk; if none arrives, _arm_history_wave_watchdog and _finalize_stranded_history_wave should cancel, advance generation, and report the shortfall. The watchdog/shortfall path did what was expected. This finding is the live data-path failure/stall; root cause is not established.
Severity: observability only (the history gap is real, but it was reported rather than silently presented as complete).
Minimal reproduction: SPY / 1 min / ETH on / empty cache / live account on 127.0.0.1:7496 / two 30 D plans; at +60 s wave state queue=0, in-flight={2,5}, generation=1.
Suggested fix / next action: Do not increase the watchdog or label the wave complete. First inspect an enabled TWS API/HMDS log or obtain the server-side trace for reqIds 2 and 5; the app observed HMDS farm “OK” at handshake (APP L135) and realtime bars worked on reqId=4, but there is no API-specific TWS log available in this run. Re-run a bounded market-data-only request after the TWS/HMDS cause is known. If the same no-bar/no-END behavior recurs, retain the watchdog and ensure the per-reqId timeout/shortfall remains visible. No code-level cause can be claimed from this evidence alone.

2. In-flight backfill progress disappeared from the status bar while requests were still outstanding.
What happened: At 13:22:04.195 the app showed “backfilling 2 historical chunk(s)…”. At 13:22:04.269, “Account summary received for SPY” replaced it (4 s timeout). At +60 s, immediately before reload, the status bar was blank while reqIds 2 and 5 were still in flight and cache count was 0. The coalesced reload then showed the expected coalesced message. Evidence: reqId=2,5; OBS L38-L49, L80-L83.
Expected behavior: users probing a stalled history path should be able to distinguish an active backfill from a finished/cache-current chart; the status should not be the only transient indicator if unrelated status messages can overwrite it.
Severity: observability only.
Minimal reproduction: same inputs as finding 1; allow the first status message to be overwritten by an unrelated status while the wave remains in-flight.
Suggested fix: Provide a persistent backfill-state indicator separate from the transient status bar (e.g. active request count, queue count, elapsed time, and current reqIds); keep the transient bar for one-shot notices. Clear the persistent indicator only on normal completion, stranded finalization, cancellation, or disconnect. Do not show “Cache current” until the cache/planner state actually supports it.
