- **The merge fetches nothing beyond the union, and loses nothing.** Every span still enumerates *all* its own chunk anchors (the inner `for _ in range(chunk_count)` loop and `anchor -= chunk_seconds` advance are untouched, `:637-656`); because anchors step by a fixed chunk from each span's own end, the union of plans is exactly the union of per-span coverage, and each span's expanded coverage is `[end − n·chunk, end] ⊇ span`. A shorter span processed first at a shared anchor is the only case that could truncate, and it cannot: the longer span continues past the shared anchors to its own earlier ones, so the shared anchor's plan plus the longer span's remaining anchors reproduce the longer coverage. This is what `test_p27_t1_02_overlap_keeps_floor_coverage` checks — and it derives the expected anchor set with its **own** chunk arithmetic rather than calling the implementation, so the assertion cannot be a tautology.

### P27-T2 — D78: the deferral backstop can no longer pre-empt its own retry

- **`retry_ms` is computed once and both timers read it.** `retry_ms = max(1000, int(wait_s * 1000))` (`ui/ticker_session.py:1827`), then `self._prime_timer.start(_prime_backstop_ms(retry_ms))` (`:1828`) and `QTimer.singleShot(retry_ms, self._on_prime_retry)` (`:1829`). `_prime_backstop_ms = max(_PRIME_BACKSTOP_MS, retry_ms + _PRIME_RETRY_GRACE_MS)` (`:76-87`) gives 15 000 / 16 000 / 41 000 / 601 000 for 2 s / 15 s / 40 s / 600 s — the arithmetic the fence pins independently (`test_prime_backstop_tracks_the_retry_deadline_at_long_waits`) and the suite pins behaviourally against a fake clock.
